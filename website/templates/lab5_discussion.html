Tables and constraints

REVIEW connects a member to a book. Its primary key is review_id. Foreign keys require an existing book and member. CHECK restricts rating to 1–5. UNIQUE(book_id, member_id) permits one review from a member per book. Deleting a book cascades to its reviews and loans. Deleting an author cascades through their books. Referenced members, publishers and categories use restrictive foreign keys.

F2.1 · Above the library average

A scalar subquery calculates AVG(price). The outer query keeps prices above that value and sorts highest first. It displays that same library average in each row.

WHERE d.price > (SELECT AVG(price) FROM BOOK)

F2.2 · Never borrowed

NOT EXISTS checks that no matching loan row exists for the current book or member. It returns the same IDs as Lab 4's LEFT JOIN with loan_id IS NULL. A returned loan still counts as borrowing history.

F2.3 · Best of the category

The inner query uses the outer book's category_id, so it is correlated. MAX finds the most expensive price in that category. Every book tied at that price appears. The second query compares a book's price with AVG(price) in its own category. A one-book category has no book above its own average.

F2.4 · Active members

A derived table in FROM first counts loans for each member. LEFT JOIN and COUNT(loan_id) give zero to members without loans. AVG then averages those counts. HAVING keeps members above that average. EXISTS shows each author once if at least one of their books has a loan.

Fresh seed example — 12 loans across 5 members gives 2.40. Reader A has 5 and Reader B has 3, so both qualify.

F2.5 · Reviews

LEFT JOIN keeps every book. COUNT(review_id) gives 0 for an unreviewed book, while COALESCE(AVG(rating), 0) displays 0. CASE checks No review first, then Excellent at 4.5+, Good at 3.5+, and Fair otherwise. HAVING compares each book's average with AVG(rating) across individual review rows.

Fresh seed examples — Palace Walk is Excellent at 4.50, Sugar Street is Good at 3.50, The Map of Love is Fair at 2.00, and Animal Farm has No review. All nine ratings average 3.6667. This overall average is calculated across reviews, so books with more reviews contribute more ratings.

F2.6 · Countries and people

UNION combines author and publisher countries and removes duplicates. INTERSECT keeps countries present in both. EXCEPT keeps author countries absent from publishers. These native operators need MySQL 8.0.31 or later. UNION ALL combines people with an Author or Member type while preserving different people who share a name.

Bonus completed · book_details

The view is defined at the end of problem2_DDL.sql. It joins BOOK, AUTHOR, PUBLISHER and CATEGORY once. F2.1 and both F2.3 queries use FROM book_details d. A view saves the SELECT definition and reads current base-table data whenever selected.

SELECT * FROM book_details;
SHOW CREATE VIEW book_details;
